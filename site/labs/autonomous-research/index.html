An autonomous research organization doing rigorous small-model ML on an 18GB MacBook. About 30 specialist agents across research, engineering, evaluation, quality, knowledge, and meta layers. No cloud compute, no external frameworks. Tensor library, model architecture, tokenizer, training, and inference all written from scratch in C17 and Metal.

MISSION

Two missions, both load-bearing

The aspirational mission is the star polaris: beat Claude Opus on standardized benchmarks (MMLU, HumanEval, ARC-AGI, GSM8K, MATH, HellaSwag, TruthfulQA, WinoGrande, BigBench-Hard) on 18GB laptop hardware with no cloud compute. Not currently achievable at open-weights state of the art. The lab proved this formally in its first envelope paper, with 3-of-5 primary-floor hard fails and a 43.7-point GPQA Diamond gap at the 70B open-weight scale.

The aspirational mission is not revised downward. It stays fixed as a forcing function that keeps the research rigorous and frontier-facing.

The real mission is what the lab actually produces, month over month: rigorous small-model research at 100M to 1B scale, plus honest envelope papers characterizing the frontier gap. Positive-result papers on architecture, training, and efficiency at one billion parameters or less. Negative-result papers showing what laptop-scale models cannot do. Methodology papers on the lab itself as a research artifact. Reproducibility reports replicating recent small-model claims.

A number from a 5000-step run outranks a PhD's intuition, a famous paper, and the Director's preference.

Lab value · Evidence Outranks Authority

METHOD

Programs, not cycles

Work is organized into programs. Each program is a multi-cycle research arc with a pre-registered question, a defined methodology, and an end-state criterion. Programs run to completion or to a published null result; they do not get quietly abandoned. PI and Director hold co-equal authority on program selection through a unanimous-compromise gate.

Within a program, cycles dispatch specialists across the layered org. Research-layer agents read papers and synthesize methodology. Engineering-layer agents implement training infrastructure. Evaluation-layer agents run benchmarks. Quality-layer agents independently audit findings. Knowledge-layer agents maintain shared memory and cross-references. Communication-layer agents handle paper drafting, abstracts, and external write-ups for submission and publication. Meta-layer agents handle governance, anti-forgery checks, and the unanimous-compromise mediation.

All agents run on the most capable available model at maximum effort. There is no model tiering inside the lab; the bottleneck is judgment, not throughput.

OUTPUT

What gets published

The lab targets ICLR and NeurIPS workshop tier for positive small-model contributions. Envelope papers go up as the rigorous negative-result counterpart, characterizing exactly what 18GB laptops cannot reach and why. Methodology contributions on autonomous-agent research labs themselves (anti-forgery, pre-registration, unanimous compromise) are a third stream.

The lab itself is a research artifact. It documents its own failures, ratifies its own structural fixes, and ships post-mortems alongside results. The methodology is the second product.
