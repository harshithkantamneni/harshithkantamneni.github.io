Every PR, closed ones included. States checked Nov 20, 2026.

NVIDIA cuDNN frontend #2575: SM90 D128 attention kernel

A native H100 forward kernel for 128-wide heads, FP16 and BF16, causal and dense, MHA, GQA and MQA. Against cuDNN 9.27 on 96 H100 shapes, a median 1.062× causal and 1.054× dense, and faster than the frontend's earlier path on 96 of 96, a median 2.5×, in my measurements. Every causal schedule gives the one-tile kernel's output bit for bit.

Merged. Nov 14, 2026

flash-linear-attention #1412: KV-cache decoding

Added KV-cache decoding for stick-breaking attention, tested against the uncached forward. On H200 at batch 1 and 64K keys, 32 programs on 132 SMs take 4.3 ms, against a K/V read floor of about 0.06 ms at rated bandwidth.

Merged. Oct 27, 2026

flash-linear-attention #1319: stick-breaking attention in Triton

Continued #599 to add forward and backward Triton kernels. Rebuilt the backward to fix a silent zero gradient at saturated logits, caught in review. On H100, 64×32 tiles fit 57-89 KiB of shared memory where 64×64 needed 129-193 KiB. Nothing saturates in the backward at about 12% occupancy, which I read as stalls on fp32 scans. It still takes 1.04-1.42× the original authors' time, and I haven't isolated why.

Merged. Oct 8, 2026

NVIDIA cuDNN frontend #1287: GQA tests

Tests for native grouped-query attention, which catch 3 injected bugs that NVIDIA's SM80 tests missed.

Tests merged. Oct 6, 2026

NVIDIA TransformerEngine #3591: grouped NVFP4 dequantization

CUDA kernel and a fixed PyTorch binding, reading offsets on device so it runs in a CUDA graph. Output matches per-tensor results bitwise.

Open

vLLM #38338: Triton NVFP4 GEMM reference

Ordered grouped tiles and autotuned tile sizes, holding 1,014-1,076 TFLOP/s on RTX 5090 for one shape from batch 8192 up, where the first revision fell to 418. On Llama QKV with hot L2: 1.38-3.04× CUTLASS up to batch 128, 0.77-0.91× above, and about 0.27× across 4 Llama shapes on B200 from batch 1024. I closed it after the issue it answered, #21014, was closed as not planned.

Closed, not merged. Oct 7, 2026

NVIDIA cuDNN frontend #1223: native GQA prototype

On A100 at 64/8 heads and a 128K KV cache, K/V head copies took 7.28 ms against a 6.0-6.7 ms kernel. Removing them made 6 batched rows 2.6-3.3× faster, and 10 more stopped running out of memory. Those rows are really 3 configs and 5 configs. The cuDNN backend stayed faster on 27 of 31 multi-token rows. Closed after NVIDIA shipped native GQA in #1239.

Closed, not merged. Sep 27, 2026

moe-kernels: a timing model of vLLM's fused MoE kernel

Predicts how long vLLM's fused MoE kernel will take on an NVIDIA GH200 before it runs, from the layer's shape and how many tokens each expert receives. In real vLLM serving on eight MoE models it was never fit on, across 2.5 million decode calls, its average error is 1.27% to 4.04%. Frontier, a published predictor trained on each model on the same GPU, reads 2.16% to 6.68%, and a roofline 12.60% to 36.02%. On prefill Frontier is closer, 1.07% to 2.84% against 5.46% to 6.73%; a per-kernel breakdown places that gap in the matrix-multiply part of the prediction, which is the next step. Every prediction in the cell tests was public before the GPU that measured it was launched.
