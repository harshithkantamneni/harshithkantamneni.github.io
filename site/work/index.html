Write-up

moe-kernels: a timing model of vLLM's fused MoE kernel

Predicts each call's time from the routing and the tile, with predictions frozen before each rental, on rented GH200s. On one 32×64×64 test tile, model v3's time predictions held at 0.51-0.69% rms. Its byte predictions missed by 11.76% on OLMoE's first wave, and the decode blind test failed at 11.4% against a 1.5% bar. No model is blind-certified on vLLM's own tiles.

Every upstream PR, closed ones included. States checked Nov 3, 2026.

flash-linear-attention #1412: KV-cache decoding

Added KV-cache decoding for stick-breaking attention, tested against the uncached forward. On H200 at batch 1 and 64K keys, 32 programs on 132 SMs take 4.3 ms, against a K/V read floor of about 0.06 ms at rated bandwidth.

Merged. Oct 27, 2026

flash-linear-attention #1319: stick-breaking attention in Triton

Continued #599 to add forward and backward Triton kernels. Rewrote the backward to fix a silent zero gradient at saturated logits, caught in review. On H100, 64×32 tiles fit 57-89 KiB of shared memory where 64×64 needed 129-193 KiB. Nothing saturates in the backward at about 12% occupancy, which I read as stalls on fp32 scans. It still takes 1.04-1.42× the original authors' time, and I haven't isolated why.

Merged. Oct 8, 2026

NVIDIA cuDNN frontend #1287: GQA tests

Tests for native grouped-query attention, which catch 3 injected bugs that NVIDIA's SM80 tests missed.

Tests merged. Oct 6, 2026

NVIDIA TransformerEngine #3591: grouped NVFP4 dequantization

CUDA kernel and a fixed PyTorch binding, reading offsets on device so it runs in a CUDA graph. Output matches per-tensor results bitwise.

In review. Open

vLLM #38338: Triton NVFP4 GEMM reference

Ordered grouped tiles and autotuned tile sizes, holding 1,014-1,076 TFLOP/s on RTX 5090 for one shape from batch 8192 up, where the first revision fell to 418. On Llama QKV with hot L2: 1.38-3.04× CUTLASS up to batch 128, 0.77-0.91× above, and about 0.27× across 4 Llama shapes on B200 from batch 1024. I closed it after #21014 was closed as not planned.

Closed, not merged. Oct 7, 2026

NVIDIA cuDNN frontend #1223: native GQA prototype

On A100 at 64/8 heads and a 128K KV cache, K/V head copies took 7.28 ms against a 6.0-6.7 ms kernel. Removing them made 6 batched rows 2.6-3.3× faster, and 10 more stopped running out of memory. Those rows are really 3 configs and 5 configs. The cuDNN backend stayed faster on 27 of 31 multi-token rows. Closed after NVIDIA shipped native GQA in #1239.

Closed, not merged. Sep 27, 2026
